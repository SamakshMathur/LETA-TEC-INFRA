[POINT 1/10] **LETA INTERPRETATION OF USER QUERY**
The user seeks a precise legal opinion on the quantum of late fee payable under **Section 47 of the CGST Act, 2017** for failure to file GST returns by the prescribed due date. This encompasses GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 returns.

---

[POINT 2/10] **MAIN CONCLUSIVE ANSWER (EXECUTIVE SUMMARY)**
A registered person who fails to furnish returns by the due date is liable to pay a **late fee per day of default**, subject to a statutory maximum cap. The quantum varies depending on the **type of return** and **whether the return is a NIL return or carries a tax liability**. The base statutory provision prescribes **Rs. 100 per day** under Section 47, but this has been **reduced and differentiated** via Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax.

---

[POINT 3/10] **FACTUAL UNDERSTANDING / ASSUMPTIONS**
- The query pertains to late filing of GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9).
- The registered person has **not filed the return by the prescribed due date** under Section 39 or Section 44 or Section 45.
- The late fee accrues **for every day** of continued default until the return is filed.
- No assumption is made about the reason for delayed filing (no force majeure or amnesty notification is assumed to be applicable).

---

[POINT 4/10] **RELEVANT LEGAL PROVISIONS**
The following provisions are directly applicable:

| Provision | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **Section 47, CGST Act 2017** | Primary charging provision for late fee |
| **Section 39, CGST Act 2017** | Prescribes due dates for filing monthly/quarterly returns |
| **Section 44, CGST Act 2017** | Prescribes due date for Annual Return (GSTR-9) |
| **Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax** | Reduces and caps late fee differentiated by return type and NIL/non-NIL status |

---

[POINT 5/10] **VERBATIM STATUTORY EXTRACTS**

**Section 47(1) of the CGST Act, 2017** — [CGST_Act_2017.pdf](http://localhost:8000/api/documents/view#page=47):

> *"Any registered person who fails to furnish the details of outward or inward supplies required under section 37 or section 38 or returns required under section 39 or section 44 or section 45 by the due date shall pay a late fee of **one hundred rupees for every day during which such failure continues subject to a maximum amount of five thousand rupees.**"*

> ⚠️ **NOTE:** The above is the **base statutory rate**. Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax has **reduced and differentiated** the late fee caps as detailed in Point 8 below.

---

[POINT 6/10] **LEGAL ANALYSIS & ADVERSARIAL CHECK**

**➤ Trigger Condition:**
Late fee under Section 47 is triggered **automatically** upon failure to file by the due date. There is **no mens rea (intent) requirement** — the liability arises regardless of whether the default was wilful or accidental.

**➤ Accrual Mechanism:**
- Late fee accrues **for every calendar day** of continued failure.
- It continues to accrue until the **actual date of filing**, not the date of payment of late fee.

**➤ Adversarial / Blocking Considerations:**
1. **Amnesty Schemes:** The CBIC has periodically issued amnesty notifications waiving or reducing late fees for specific periods. These must be checked independently for the relevant tax period.
2. **NIL vs. Non-NIL Returns:** Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax creates a bifurcation — NIL returns attract a **significantly lower maximum cap**.
3. **GSTR-9 (Annual Return):** Late fee for GSTR-9 is calculated differently — linked to **aggregate turnover** rather than a fixed maximum.
4. **Section 47 vs. Section 50:** Late fee under Section 47 is **separate from and in addition to** interest on delayed tax payment under Section 50 (18% per annum). Both can apply simultaneously.

---

[POINT 7/10] **APPLICATION TO PRESENT CASE**

The late fee computation depends on **three determinants**:
1. **Which return** is delayed (GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B / GSTR-9)
2. **Whether** it is a NIL return or carries tax liability
3. **Number of days** of delay

The late fee is payable as follows:
- **CGST component** is paid from Electronic Cash Ledger under CGST head.
- **SGST/UTGST component** is paid under respective State/UT head.
- Late fee **cannot be paid from the Electronic Credit Ledger (ITC)** — it must be paid in **cash only** per Section 49.

[POINT 8/10] **NUMERIC DATA / RATES (TRUTH RULES)**

As per **Section 47 read with Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax**, the late fee structure is:

| Return Type | Condition | Late Fee Per Day | Maximum Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GSTR-3B** | With tax liability | **Rs. 50/day** (Rs. 25 CGST + Rs. 25 SGST) | **Rs. 5,000** |
| **GSTR-3B** | NIL return | **Rs. 20/day** (Rs. 10 CGST + Rs. 10 SGST) | **Rs. 500** |
| **GSTR-1** | With liability | **Rs. 50/day** (Rs. 25 CGST + Rs. 25 SGST) | **Rs. 10,000** |
| **GSTR-1** | NIL return | **Rs. 20/day** (Rs. 10 CGST + Rs. 10 SGST) | **Rs. 500** |
| **GSTR-9** | Annual return | **Rs. 200/day** (Rs. 100 CGST + Rs. 100 SGST) | **0.25% of aggregate turnover** in the State |

> 🔴 **IMPORTANT:** The **base statutory rate of Rs. 100/day** under Section 47 is for a **single Act (CGST)**. The **combined rate (CGST + SGST)** is therefore **Rs. 200/day** under the bare Act — but Notification No. 19/2021 has **capped and reduced** this for practical compliance as shown above.

[POINT 9/10] **RELEVANT NOTIFICATIONS / CIRCULARS / PRECEDENTS**

| Reference | Key Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax** | Differentiates and reduces late fee caps for GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 |
| **Section 50(1), CGST Act** | Interest at **18% per annum** applies separately on delayed tax payment — cumulative with Section 47 late fee |
| **Section 49, CGST Act** | Late fee must be paid through Electronic **Cash** Ledger — ITC cannot be used |

[POINT 10/10] **FINAL TAX POSITION & CAVEATS**

**✅ FINAL POSITION:**
Late fee under Section 47 is a **mandatory statutory levy** triggered automatically on delayed filing. The applicable rate (as reduced by Notification No. 19/2021-Central Tax) is:
- **Rs. 50/day** for GSTR-3B/GSTR-1 with liability (max Rs. 5,000 / Rs. 10,000 respectively)
- **Rs. 20/day** for NIL returns (max Rs. 500)
- **Rs. 200/day** for GSTR-9 (max 0.25% of aggregate turnover)

**⚠️ CAVEATS:**
1. Late fee is **over and above** interest under Section 50 — both apply simultaneously.
2. Late fee is **payable in cash only** — ITC cannot be utilised.
3. Any **period-specific amnesty notification** must be independently verified from the official [CBIC portal](https://cbic.gov.in) for the relevant tax period.
4. For **IGST-only registrations**, only the IGST component of late fee applies.
5. This opinion is based on the statutory text and Truth Rules available in the documentation. For any period-specific waiver or amnesty, **official CBIC notifications must be checked independently.**
